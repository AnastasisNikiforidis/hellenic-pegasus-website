About us

Who we are

Hellenic Pegasus is the Anatolia College VEX V5 Robotics Team. We are nine members who design, build and program a competition robot from scratch, and we are the only VEX V5 team in Greece.

Our name comes from Pegasus, the winged horse of Greek mythology. Our logo shows it rising from a gear: ancient inspiration, modern engineering.

This season we compete in Override, the 2026–2027 VEX V5 game. Because no other team in Greece plays V5, our path leads straight to the World Championship.

Our goals

  • Represent Greece with pride on the world stage.
  • Build a reliable, well-engineered robot and a consistent autonomous routine.
  • Grow every member’s skills in engineering, programming, design and teamwork.
  • Inspire more students in Greece to discover robotics.

What we expect at Worlds

  • To compete with confidence and give every match our best.
  • To learn from the strongest teams in the world and bring that knowledge home.
  • To be friendly, respectful partners to every alliance we play with.
  • To return with new ideas, new friends and a stronger team.

Ready to drive?

Score with the red robot. Beat the blue robot before time runs out.


Keys: W A S D or arrows drive. Space grabs or places a Pin. F flips your top Pin. E sets the nearest Toggle to red. C changes the camera.

5A red or blue Pin in a Quadrant of its color, or in the Midfield.

10A yellow Pin you own: its Quadrant’s Toggle matches your color. Midfield yellow Pins go to the alliance with more robots there.

8Each of your robots in the Midfield when the match ends.

Simplified for the web. Goals, Toggles and Pin spots follow VEX’s official Override field coordinates, and the colors come from the official field render. Loader spots, Cups, Goal sizes, the blue robot and the Midfield size are approximations. Check the official Game Manual for exact rules.
